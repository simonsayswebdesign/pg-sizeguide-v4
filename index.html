Precision Fitting

HOW TO TAKE ACCURATE MEASUREMENTS

Taking exact measurements ensures everyone on your crew receives garments that fit comfortably throughout the working day.

C

Chest Measurement

Measure around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the measuring tape horizontal, relaxed and level under the armpits.

W

Waist Measurement

Measure around your natural waistline, where you normally wear your work trousers or shorts. Do not pull the tape too tight.

L

Inside Leg (Inseam)

Measure from the top of the crotch seam straight down the inside seam to the hem at your ankle bone, or measure well-fitting trousers.

G

Garment Silhouette

Check whether the product is Unisex Regular, Fitted/Ladies, or Oversized Outerwear before finalizing your order quantity.

Workplace Uniform Advice

EXPERT FIT & EMBROIDERY TIPS

Custom embroidered clothing cannot usually be returned once decorated. Follow these rules for a smooth team rollout.

Between Sizes? Size Up

If your measurement falls between two standard sizes, we always advise choosing the larger size. Physical trade jobs require extra freedom of movement, and tight garments can distort left-chest embroidery.

Layering Allowances

Outerwear such as softshell jackets, winter parkas and fleece zip-ups are typically worn over hoodies or heavy work polos. Order jackets one size larger if you plan on heavy winter layering on site.

Order Plain Sample Packs

For large workforce uniform renewals (15+ wearers), request a plain sizing pack from our Langwith studio first. Your staff can try on actual garments before we start bulk embroidery and print runs.
